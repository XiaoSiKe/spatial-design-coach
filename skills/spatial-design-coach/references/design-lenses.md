# Design Lenses and Reflective Practice

Read only when a source or philosophical question can change the current design decision. Select at most 2–3 relevant lenses, normally a useful proposal and a counterpoint. A book name is not proof and its visual signature is not a design instruction.

## Use sources at their actual depth

The B numbers preserve the user's 50-book bibliography; 24 books are selected below, eight per discipline. Z01 adds selected Zhuangzi passages. **E** means an identified original excerpt or preface was inspected; **M** means introductory, catalogue, or bibliographic material only. Neither means a whole book was read. All **Teaching move** and **Test / limit** statements are this project's adaptations, not quotations or empirical findings attributed to an author.

When using a card, name the relevant idea and the inspected source level if attribution matters. Give exact quotations or page references only after checking the actual passage and edition. For M sources, keep attribution at the documented topic level; verify the chapter before assigning a specific argument to its author. Do not turn a book, an evocative metaphor, or a proposed comparison into evidence about the student's site.

Distinguish the student's material from this Skill's bibliographic records. If the student supplies only a book introduction, their edition and requested passage remain unknown. Usually omit unrelated years and ISBNs; if a catalogue record matters, name/link that record and say it identifies an available edition, not the student's copy or an inspected chapter. Use a concise origin statement, for example: “你提供的是书介；以下是我的教学建议；性能主张仍待场地验证。”

In the student-facing reply, explicitly distinguish the source topic from your teaching adaptation and from the project claim to be tested. A short statement of origin is enough; fixed headings are unnecessary. When comparing lenses, state what one challenges in the other under a shared criterion.

## Start with the live tension

| Tension | Useful sources | Counter-question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear order and complex use | B02, B03, B17 | Which real activity is a tidy composition excluding? |
| Belonging and access | B04, B18, B19 | Whose experience differs, and can they still enter and choose how to use it? |
| Public vitality and continuity | B25, B32, B33, B35 | Who maintains the place or loses a valued use through this change? |
| Ecology and inhabitation | B37, B42, B45, B47 | Which process is protected, at what cost to access or stewardship? |
| Local form and larger systems | B21, B26, B27, B50 | What happens beyond the site boundary and over time? |
| Intuition and accountable practice | B06, B10, B39, B49, Z01 | What can the next small test establish, and what remains outside our knowledge? |

Translate through **source idea → situated tension → student value choice → spatial move → test**. Keep observations, value preferences, and technical claims distinct. Values guide the choice among defensible alternatives; they cannot waive brief requirements, verified constraints, or a locked decision. Any reopening of a locked choice still follows the existing student-confirmation rule.

## Reflect on life through practice

Use one optional question only when it helps the current bottleneck, within the ordinary question budget: what way of living does this proposal support, whose different life is overlooked, or what did this attempt teach you? A student may answer through an imagined user, a drawing, or a project example. Personal history is not required. If reflection is declined, continue spatial coaching immediately.

During discouragement, acknowledge the experience first and distinguish the person's worth from a testable issue in the work. Do not prescribe acceptance of humiliation, diagnose character, or use philosophy to erase valid criticism. In rescue mode, return to the existing three work packages; philosophical reflection must not add a reading assignment, fourth package, or reason to restart a locked scheme.

Use existing project-state fields for any student-confirmed value, rationale, trade-off, source status, and next Artifact. Do not store emotional disclosures, life stories, or inferred beliefs. End a completed project with a reusable observation or test the student can carry to another project.

## Architecture: eight selected books

<a id="b02"></a>
### B02 · 《建筑：形式、空间和秩序》 — 程大锦

- **Basis (M):** Form and space as a vocabulary for describing architectural relationships; [Wiley / Google Books, fifth English edition](https://books.google.com/books?id=aNy4EAAAQBAJ).
- **Teaching move:** Ask what encloses, connects, or distinguishes the spaces, then change one relationship before adding surface detail.
- **Test / limit:** Locate the same enclosure and route in a plan and section. Geometric clarity does not establish comfort or belonging; counter with B04 or B19.

<a id="b03"></a>
### B03 · 《建筑空间组合论》 — 彭一刚

- **Basis (M):** The catalogue describes function, structure, composition, interior space, and exterior form; [third-edition library record](https://opaclib.lut.edu.cn/opac/book/b7df1b3545b921f48714f1b0d3021c8a).
- **Teaching move:** Compare two ways of grouping the same activities through adjacency, hierarchy, and sequence.
- **Test / limit:** Explain one consequence of each arrangement in linked plan/section diagrams. A successful composition still needs evidence of use and technical feasibility; counter with B06 and B10.

<a id="b04"></a>
### B04 · 《建筑学教程1：设计原理》 — 赫尔曼·赫茨伯格

- **Basis (M):** The author's bibliography identifies *Lessons for Students in Architecture* and its Chinese translation; the user's bibliography identifies use and public/private transitions as the reading focus. [Author's record](https://www.hertzberger.nl/index.php/en/publicaties-van-herman-hertzberger/238-lessons-for-students-in-architecture).
- **Teaching move:** Treat an entrance or threshold as a place where access, responsibility, and opportunities for appropriation change.
- **Test / limit:** Draw who may pass, pause, watch, or withdraw on both sides. A shared threshold must not force interaction or erase necessary privacy; counter with B06.

<a id="b06"></a>
### B06 · 《公共建筑设计原理》 — 张文忠等

- **Basis (M):** Public-building layout, functional relationships, technology/economy, spatial synthesis, and accessibility are listed in the [fifth-edition book description](https://books.google.com/books?id=f9kH0AEACAAJ).
- **Teaching move:** Trace users, staff, service, and exceptional events before accepting a spatial arrangement.
- **Test / limit:** Identify one conflict and compare ways of resolving it without erasing the programme. Diagrammatic separation does not prove code compliance; current requirements and specialist checks remain necessary.

<a id="b10"></a>
### B10 · 《图解建筑结构：模式、体系与设计》 — 程大锦等

- **Basis (M):** Structural systems are treated as part of building design; [Chinese publisher record](https://www.tjupress.com.cn/book/detail/52C69306-733A-452E-8417-2BFF77810CB6) and [Wiley's second English edition](https://uat.store.wiley.com/en-us/building-structures-illustrated-patterns-systems-and-design-2nd-edition-p-9781118458358).
- **Teaching move:** Connect the desired room or opening to a plausible load path, support layout, and structural depth.
- **Test / limit:** Trace forces and supports through the same section used for space. This is a conceptual check, never an invented member size, capacity calculation, or engineering approval.

<a id="b17"></a>
### B17 · 《建筑的复杂性与矛盾性》 — 罗伯特·文丘里

- **Basis (E):** The inspected manifesto and discussion of simplification distinguish inclusive complexity from arbitrary incoherence; [MoMA's original-text sample, chapters 1–2](https://www.moma.org/d/pdfs/W1siZiIsIjIwMTkvMDcvMDEvOHI4MnI1aW1qcV9XZWJTYW1wbGVfQ29tcGxleGl0eV9Wb2xfMWFuZDIucGRmIl1d/WebSample_Complexity_Vol_1and2.pdf?sha=de7bd6b30f97ab4e).
- **Teaching move:** Name the two real demands a single element must accommodate, then compare adaptation with deliberate separation.
- **Test / limit:** Show both demands in use and explain the overall order. Complexity is not permission for contradictory drawings, unsupported claims, or unresolved circulation; counter with B02 and B06.

<a id="b18"></a>
### B18 · 《建筑模式语言》 — 克里斯托弗·亚历山大等

- **Basis (M):** The author's site introduces related patterns across towns, buildings, and construction; [book introduction](https://www.patternlanguage.com/labyrinth/apl-tour1.html).
- **Teaching move:** Identify a recurring activity conflict and propose a relationship that could resolve it under this site's conditions.
- **Test / limit:** Name what changes locally and a case where the pattern would fail. A pattern is a provisional aid, not a universally correct dimension, behaviour, or template; counter with B27 and B35.

<a id="b19"></a>
### B19 · 《场所精神：迈向建筑现象学》 — 克里斯蒂安·诺伯舒兹

- **Basis (M):** *Genius Loci* relates architectural understanding to concrete existence; [Rizzoli 1980 catalogue and description](https://books.google.com/books?id=FlYkAQAAMAAJ).
- **Teaching move:** Ask which relation among ground, light, boundaries, orientation, and everyday activity helps someone recognize and inhabit this place.
- **Test / limit:** Translate belonging into one observable spatial sequence, including a contrasting user's experience. Do not assume a single authentic local identity or solicit personal memories; counter with B31 and B33.

## Urban planning: eight selected books

<a id="b21"></a>
### B21 · 《城市规划原理》 — 吴志强、李德华

- **Basis (M):** The supplied bibliography's planning-framework focus is aligned with the [fourth-edition professional textbook listing](https://www.ccen.com.cn/info/1150/2444.htm); chapter-level arguments have not been inspected.
- **Teaching move:** Connect land use, services, transport, environment, and implementation through one consequential planning decision.
- **Test / limit:** Trace an affected group, a spatial allocation, and a dependency beyond the site. Historical textbook categories do not establish today's planning law or local requirements; use current sources when those matter.

<a id="b25"></a>
### B25 · 《城市设计》 — 王建国

- **Basis (M):** The author's university describes theory, methods, scales, digital approaches, and implementation; [fourth-edition record](https://updi.seu.edu.cn/2023/0512/c46557a447253/page.htm).
- **Teaching move:** Connect block form, street section, building edge, and public space through one urban-design proposition.
- **Test / limit:** Use a shared section or route to show the local and urban consequences. Digital analysis and formal coherence do not prove social benefit; counter with B32 and B35.

<a id="b26"></a>
### B26 · 《详细规划》 — 阳建强

- **Basis (M):** The university's description covers residential areas, centres, historic districts, transport, municipal systems, and teaching examples; [2019 textbook record](https://updi.seu.edu.cn/2023/0525/c44377a446669/page.htm).
- **Teaching move:** Translate the assignment's confirmed planning requirements into spatial controls and check their joint effect in a focus area.
- **Test / limit:** A control diagram and section must describe the same capacity and access assumptions. Verify applicable contemporary standards instead of treating a textbook scheme as a current legal approval.

<a id="b27"></a>
### B27 · 《乡村规划原理》 — 李京生

- **Basis (M):** The supplied reading focus is rural settlements and production/living relations; a contributing author's [Tongji bibliography](https://upd-caup.tongji.edu.cn/ghz/list.htm) confirms the 2019 textbook. Specific chapter claims remain unverified.
- **Teaching move:** Map everyday production, residence, public services, and seasonal use before choosing a village intervention.
- **Test / limit:** Show how a retained livelihood connects to a public space or route. Do not invent local customs, impose tourism as the default economy, or transplant an urban pattern unchanged; counter with B18.

<a id="b31"></a>
### B31 · 《城市意象》 — 凯文·林奇

- **Basis (M):** Imageability and how residents understand city form are the documented focus; [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262620017/the-image-of-the-city/).
- **Teaching move:** Compare how different users might recognize a route and where additional observation is needed.
- **Test / limit:** Make a provisional route/mental-map comparison and mark what has actually been observed. Recognition is distinct from welcome, comfort, or access; counter with B19 and B33.

<a id="b32"></a>
### B32 · 《美国大城市的死与生》 — 简·雅各布斯

- **Basis (M):** The publisher foregrounds urban diversity, street life, and the effects of planning; [Modern Library edition](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/86058/the-death-and-life-of-great-american-cities-by-jane-jacobs/).
- **Teaching move:** Follow who uses the same street at different times and what an intervention preserves or removes from those relationships.
- **Test / limit:** Compare time-of-day use and access under two proposals. Street vitality alone does not establish affordability or protection from displacement; counter with B35 and B21.

<a id="b33"></a>
### B33 · 《交往与空间》 — 扬·盖尔

- **Basis (E):** The opening excerpt distinguishes necessary, optional, and social outdoor activities and qualifies the role of physical conditions; [English text, chapter 1, pp. 9–12](https://cus.ubt-uni.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Jan-Gehl-Life-Between-Buildings_-Using-Public-Space-2011-Island-Press.pdf).
- **Teaching move:** Ask what permits a person to remain voluntarily, interact, or leave without pressure.
- **Test / limit:** Compare an edge or seating sequence across weather, time, and use. An invitation does not guarantee sociability; keep unobserved behaviour provisional and counter with B32's broader street conditions.

<a id="b35"></a>
### B35 · 《历史城市保护学导论》 — 张松

- **Basis (M):** The publisher-supplied description centres cultural heritage and integrated protection; [second-edition textbook record](https://aijiaocai.com/textbook/details?textbook_id=562366).
- **Teaching move:** Identify what carries continuity: fabric, spatial relationships, ongoing uses, and the people able to sustain them.
- **Test / limit:** Compare retention, adaptation, and replacement with reasons and affected users. Retaining a façade does not prove cultural continuity; statutory protection status requires independent verification.

## Landscape architecture: eight selected books

<a id="b37"></a>
### B37 · 《景观设计学：场地规划与设计手册》 — 约翰·O·西蒙兹、巴里·W·斯塔克

- **Basis (M):** Site/environmental planning, design, and implementation are integrated in the [McGraw Hill fifth English edition](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/mhp/product/landscape-architecture-fifth-edition.html).
- **Teaching move:** Place human activity, landform, water, vegetation, and movement on the same transect before resolving isolated features.
- **Test / limit:** Identify one conflict between those systems and compare its consequences across plan and section. Site-level synthesis cannot stand in for measured ecological or engineering performance.

<a id="b39"></a>
### B39 · 《从概念到形式》 — 格兰特·W·里德

- **Basis (M):** The inspected description and contents link conceptual thinking to spatial form and its development; [Wiley, second English edition](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/engineering/architecture-planning-10ar/landscape-design-10ar5/from-concept-to-form-in-landscape-design-978-0-470-11231-1).
- **Teaching move:** State an activity or environmental relation before exploring two geometric or naturalistic organizations that could support it.
- **Test / limit:** Keep the same functional requirements visible through each sketch. A flowing outline is not evidence of ecological flow; counter with B45 and B49.

<a id="b41"></a>
### B41 · 《中国古典园林分析》 — 彭一刚

- **Basis (M):** The university reading guide relates garden analysis to spatial composition, philosophy, and aesthetics; [course bibliography and book description](https://library.xujc.cn/_upload/article/files/95/99/1bf2797c4080b41e62d0214c73bc/b3e5e5ae-56cc-4238-8439-167204bad16b.pdf).
- **Teaching move:** Trace how a route changes what is visible, concealed, near, and distant; compare the experience at different stopping points.
- **Test / limit:** Link viewpoint diagrams to the actual plan and section. A private historical garden is not a ready-made model of contemporary public access; counter with B33 and B50.

<a id="b42"></a>
### B42 · 《园冶注释》 — 计成著、陈植注释

- **Basis (M):** The inspected library description identifies siting, garden buildings, rocks, and borrowed scenery; [Chinese Architecture & Building Press, annotated edition](https://www.szlib.com/DR/LocalBibliographies/Content/6563). Ancient text and modern annotation are distinct sources.
- **Teaching move:** Inventory what the site offers before adding form; use one existing landform, tree, view, or seasonal relation to change the proposal.
- **Test / limit:** Show what is retained, altered, and borrowed, including access and maintenance consequences. A natural appearance is not proof of ecological performance; counter with B45 and B49.

<a id="b45"></a>
### B45 · 《设计结合自然》 — 伊恩·麦克哈格

- **Basis (M):** Penn's McHarg Center explains ecological processes, landscape character, and suitability as a planning concern; [institutional interpretation of the book](https://mcharg.upenn.edu/conversations/what-does-it-mean-design-nature-now). This is contextual commentary, not inspected book text.
- **Teaching move:** Identify which environmental process should constrain intervention and what evidence could support that priority.
- **Test / limit:** Trace water, soil, or habitat across a shared section with human access. Suitability layers still contain value choices, data uncertainty, and social costs; counter with B32 and B50.

<a id="b47"></a>
### B47 · 《植物造景》 — 苏雪痕

- **Basis (M):** The supplied bibliography identifies planting as the focus; [FAO AGRIS / National Agricultural Library](https://agris.fao.org/search/en/providers/122535/records/65de0cf60f3e94b9e5c9d467) verifies the 1994 edition, ISBN 9787503810671. Original chapters have not been inspected.
- **Teaching move:** Start from the spatial job of a planting group, then test site conditions, growth, season, and stewardship before selecting species.
- **Test / limit:** Compare a planting section at establishment and maturity using labeled assumptions. Do not invent local suitability, root depth, maintenance capacity, or ecological benefits; obtain relevant local evidence.

<a id="b49"></a>
### B49 · 《风景园林工程》 — 孟兆祯

- **Basis (M):** The library record describes terrain, water/drainage, paths, planting, and related engineering; [Chinese Forestry Press, 2012, ISBN 9787503865190](https://space.zjgsu.edu.cn/mspace/searchDetailLocal/m66b80b6a055004bb8568b0c8b267b3b5). Original chapters and technical rules remain unverified.
- **Teaching move:** Reconcile the intended experience with terrain, drainage routes, construction interfaces, and maintenance access.
- **Test / limit:** Draw one indexed section through the conflicting systems and label unknown levels and capacities. A conceptual drawing is not a verified drainage calculation or construction detail; route specialist work when needed.

<a id="b50"></a>
### B50 · 《城市绿地系统规划》 — 许浩等

- **Basis (E):** The publisher's preface discusses the shift from individual gardens to urban/regional systems; the contents identify monitoring, suitability, and accessibility. [Tsinghua University Press, 2020 edition](https://www.tup.tsinghua.edu.cn/bookscenter/book_06590201.html).
- **Teaching move:** Connect a local green space to the network of access, ecological processes, management, and uneven provision around it.
- **Test / limit:** Trace one proposed connection and identify who or what is served and what remains disconnected. More mapped greenery does not establish equitable access or habitat continuity; counter with B33 and B45.

## Z01 · 《庄子》选篇: perspective, practice, and usefulness

These interpretations are optional teaching adaptations. The composite text has multiple interpretive traditions; do not present a single modern design doctrine as Zhuangzi's intended meaning. [Scholarly context](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/zhuangzi/).

Make the origin explicit in a reply, including when declining a misuse: “选篇涉及判断与立场；把它转成不同使用者的流线比较，是我的教学转译。入口冲突仍须用图纸检验。” Adapt the example to the actual request. “The text supports perspective changes” alone leaves the design interpretation's authorship unclear.

- **《齐物论》 (E):** The inspected discussion of settled judgments and differing standpoints prompts asking how another person frames the problem. Separate observable facts from competing priorities, then compare a spatial consequence under shared criteria. It does not establish that all factual claims or designs are equally valid. [Original passage](https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/adjustment-of-controversies/zh#n2726).
- **《养生主》 (E):** In the Cook Ding passage, practiced responsiveness includes slowing down at difficulty. Ask where the current move encounters a real constraint and what a smaller test could reveal. Do not equate mastery with intuition beyond verification, accept exhaustion as a virtue, or substitute the story for a technical calculation. [Original passage](https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/nourishing-the-lord-of-life/zh#n2735).
- **《逍遥游》 (E):** The inspected gourd/tree discussion changes the frame in which something counts as useful. As a teaching adaptation, reconsider whose definition excludes resting, play, habitat, or future adaptation; compare retaining that possibility with an alternative and name the cost. “Unused” never means automatically valuable or exempt from access and safety requirements. [Original text, final two dialogues](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=莊子/逍遙遊&oldid=7908417).

For example, when a student wants to keep an unprogrammed garden corner, ask which people or processes need that openness and compare it with a programmed corner. Request one plan/section or observation that could distinguish their consequences. The student decides which trade-off to accept; record only that project decision and the next test.
